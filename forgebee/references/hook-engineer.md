## The Hook Formula Library

| # | Category | Mechanism | Templates |
|---|----------|-----------|-----------|
| 1 | Curiosity gap | Opens a loop the brain needs to close | "The reason [common thing] doesn't work (and what to do instead)..." · "Most people get [topic] completely wrong. Here's why..." · "What [successful company] knows about [topic] that you don't..." |
| 2 | Contrarian / hot take | Cognitive dissonance against consensus | "Unpopular opinion: [bold statement]" · "Stop [common practice]. It's hurting your [goal]." · "I stopped [popular thing] and [surprising result] happened." |
| 3 | Pain point | Hits a daily nerve | "Tired of [specific frustration]? You're not alone." · "The real reason you're stuck at [plateau]..." · "You're losing [valuable thing] every time you [common mistake]." |
| 4 | Proof-first / results | Credibility and outcome up front | "I [result] in [timeframe]. Here's the exact process..." · "This one change increased our [metric] by [%]." · "From [bad state] to [good state] in [timeframe] — step by step." |
| 5 | Pattern interrupt | Something unexpected breaks the scroll | "[Single shocking word]." (then elaborate) · "Delete this from your [tool/routine] right now." · "I wasn't going to share this, but..." |
| 6 | Question | Forces mental engagement | "What would you do if [scenario]?" · "Which of these [mistakes] are you making?" · "Be honest: when did you last [desirable action]?" |
| 7 | Statistical / data | A number that makes people pause | "[Stat]% of [group] don't know this about [topic]." · "I analyzed [N] [things] and found [pattern]." · "[N] out of [N] [people] get this wrong." |
| 8 | "I spent X on Y" | Authority through personal investment | "I spent [time/money] on [thing] so you don't have to." · "I tested [N] [tools] — only [n] actually work." · "I read [N] books on [topic]. Here are the [n] ideas that matter." |
| 9 | List / framework | Promises organized, actionable value | "[N] things I wish I knew about [topic] [time] ago." · "The [N]-step framework for [outcome]." · "[N] signs you're [problem] (and how to fix each)." |
| 10 | Story / narrative | Pulls people into a story | "[Time] ago, I [relatable starting point]..." · "The worst [professional moment] of my life taught me [lesson]." · "Nobody believed me when I said [prediction]. Then..." |
| 11 | FOMO | Urgency or exclusivity | "Most people will scroll past this. The ones who don't will [benefit]." · "This strategy works right now, but it won't last." · "Your competitors are already doing this. Are you?" |
| 12 | "Nobody talks about" / secret | Insider knowledge | "The [topic] that nobody talks about..." · "The dirty secret about [industry]..." · "The unspoken rule of [domain]..." |

## Cialdini's 7 Principles Applied to Hooks

| Principle | Application | Example |
|-----------|-------------|---------|
| Reciprocity | Give value first | "Free framework: [value]. Save this." |
| Commitment | Get a small yes first | "Agree? Then you'll love what's next..." |
| Social proof | Show others doing it | "10,000+ people used this to [result]" |
| Authority | Lead with credentials | "After 10 years in [field], here's..." |
| Liking | Relatable and authentic | "I failed at this for years. Then..." |
| Scarcity | Limit availability | "I rarely share this, but..." |
| Unity | Tribe identity | "If you're a [specific identity], this is for you." |

## Hook-Retain-Reward (Hormozi)

- **Hook (1-3 s):** bold statement, surprising stat, or pain question. Earns the next 5 seconds.
- **Retain (middle):** deliver on the promise; open loops; short chunks; pattern interrupts where attention drops.
- **Reward (end):** pay off the curiosity; one actionable takeaway; CTA (save, share, follow, click).

## Platform-Specific Hook Adaptation

| Platform | Hook Length | Constraint | Best Types |
|----------|-----------|------------|------------|
| LinkedIn | 2 lines above the fold | Earn the "see more" click | Contrarian, Proof, Story |
| X | 1 post | Earn the thread click | Statistical, Question, FOMO |
| Instagram | First 1-2 s (Reels) / first line | Visual and text align | Pattern interrupt, Pain point |
| TikTok | First 1-2 s | Visual movement + spoken word | Pattern interrupt, Curiosity |
| YouTube | First 10 s | Thumbnail is the first hook | Proof, Curiosity, Promise |
| Email | Subject line | Earn the open | Curiosity, Personal, FOMO |
